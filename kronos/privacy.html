Privacy policy

Kronos, a personal home assistant. Last updated November 19, 2026.

Who uses Kronos

Kronos is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data Kronos accesses

With the owner's permission, Kronos has read-only access to the owner's Google Tasks (the tasks.readonly permission), so it can read out which tasks are due.

How the data is used and stored

  • Tasks are read only to tell the owner about them, by voice, at home.
  • The data stays on the owner's own device. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else.
  • Kronos cannot change or delete tasks.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the Google Account settings, under Third-party apps and services.
